# Privacy Policy — Farsi Be Gone

Farsi Be Gone runs entirely in your browser. It reads the text of posts on
linkedin.com only to decide whether they are written in Farsi, and hides those posts.

- It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or browsing data.
- It makes no network requests.
- The only data saved is a single on/off preference, stored with `chrome.storage.sync`
  (synced by Chrome across your own devices).
- No analytics, ads, or third-party services are used.

Contact: open an issue at https://github.com/mehdipiraee/farsi-be-gone/issues
